About Netcore

Who we are.
What we do.

Netcore AI is an agentic marketing and customer engagement platform. It brings customer signals, audiences, journeys and messaging channels together, so teams can run personalised engagement across the customer lifecycle. [5]

Netcore describes AI agents for specific marketing tasks, each operating within guardrails and business context. For a bank, the relevant capabilities are the ones this page focuses on: journey orchestration, channel delivery, audience building and optimisation.

Growth & customer experience

Help customers progress.
Give teams a clearer workflow.

Shared outcomes to test—not assumptions about existing pain points or promised uplift. DBS’s public materials describe a digital-first, AI-led approach, including 1.5 million active digiWealth users and a digibot that resolved nine in ten queries digitally in the first half of 2026. [2] [3]

Improve customer progression

Link relevant signals to an approved next step. Measure completed tasks and first use of features, not sends alone.

Keep communication coherent

Apply one set of contact limits and suppression rules across channels so completed actions stop further prompts.

Reduce avoidable operational work

Compare the effort to build, approve, change and monitor a selected journey. Identify which handoffs or repeated tasks could actually be removed.

Consolidate where practical

Validate which campaign, delivery or personalisation functions can move into Netcore, which tools remain necessary, and whether total cost supports the change.

Illustrative workflows to evaluate—not descriptions of live DBS campaigns. The proposed Netcore role is to run agreed journeys using authorised data and approved rules.

Journey 01

Help customers finish what they started.

A signal-led follow-up for a selected digital application or onboarding step, replacing the chosen follow-up process rather than adding a parallel one.

Illustrative use case
Receive

Step not completed

Map an authorised in-flow event to an identifiable, permissioned customer and check eligibility for follow-up.

Execute

Relevant next step

After an agreed wait, recheck status and contact rules, then sequence approved content on eligible channels.

Adapt

Exit on completion

Stop pending steps when the matched completion event arrives. Validate messages already in flight.

Proposed execution ownership: Netcore would run event entry, audience checks, channel delivery, contact limits, completion exits and reporting. Product flows and core banking stay in DBS systems.

Measures to agreeCompletion rate of the selected flow · time to completion · repeat-contact rate · workflow effort · event-to-action time

Journey 02

Guide customers to their first use of a digital feature.

Coordinate in-app, push and email guidance around a selected digital capability, so guidance reflects what the customer has already done.

Illustrative use case
Receive

Feature available, not yet used

Use approved eligibility rules and consent status to identify customers who could benefit from guidance.

Execute

Contextual guidance

Sequence approved in-app, push or email content, avoiding duplicate prompts across teams.

Adapt

Recognise first use

When the matched first-use event arrives, stop the onboarding prompts and move to the agreed next step.

Proposed execution ownership: Netcore would own sequencing, delivery and suppression for the selected guidance journey. DBS retains the product experience, assistants and content approval.

Measures to agreeFirst-use rate · time to first use · prompts per customer · opt-outs · journey-change effort

Journey 03

Re-engage inactive customers with relevance and restraint.

Bring a selected re-engagement programme into one coordinated sequence with clear contact limits and exits.

Illustrative use case
Receive

Inactivity threshold reached

Use approved definitions of inactivity, product rules and communication preferences.

Execute

Coordinated outreach

Apply channel eligibility, frequency caps and approved content instead of overlapping programmes.

Adapt

Stop on return

Suppress further outreach when a matched return event or another exclusion is received.

Proposed execution ownership: Netcore would own the agreed re-engagement sequence, audience, delivery and reporting. Eligibility policy and content approval stay with DBS.

Boundaries & validation

Clear ownership.
Evidence before expansion.

Permissions & contact

Confirm identity matching, consent, eligible channels and suppression rules for each journey and channel owner.

Integration & events

Identify source events, latency and ownership. Test duplicate handling, retries and exits before relying on them.

Regulatory & data controls

Validate hosting, data residency, access, retention and security against DBS’s requirements and applicable MAS expectations. Netcore publishes compliance statements; they need review for this use. [4]

Measurement & migration

Agree a baseline, a control group and complete costs. Plan cutover so old and new journeys do not run in parallel, and decide which messages stay outside scope.
